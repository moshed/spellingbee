Wordbox

Word games you play with friends, at your own pace.

Support

Questions, bugs or anything else: wordbox@dancykier.com.

Privacy

Wordbox has no accounts, no passwords and no sign-in. When you first open it, the app generates a random player id on your device and stores it in your iCloud keychain so your games survive reinstalling.

What is stored on our server:

  • your player id, the username you choose, and an optional display name
  • your scores and the words you have found in games you are playing
  • who you are friends with, and which games you are in
  • a notification token, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you when it is your turn

What is not collected: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any advertising identifier. There are no adverts, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Nothing is sold or shared.

To delete everything associated with your player id, email the address above with your username and it will be removed.
