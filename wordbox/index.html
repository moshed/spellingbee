Wordbox

Word games you play with friends, at your own pace.

Support

Questions, bugs or anything else: wordbox@dancykier.com. Include your username if you are writing about a particular game.

The games

  • Hexicon — build words from seven letters, every word using the one in the middle.
  • Anagrams — take tiles from the middle, or steal and extend a word somebody has already made.
  • Scramble — swipe words out of a letter grid against a clock, a round each.
  • Crosswords — an archive of grids to work through.

Common questions

I got a new phone — are my games gone? No. Your player id is kept in your iCloud keychain, so the same Apple Account brings every game back with nothing to sign in to.

A word I know was rejected. Every board is judged by one shared dictionary. If a real word is missing from it, email the word and it will be added.

I want my data deleted. Email the address above with your username.
